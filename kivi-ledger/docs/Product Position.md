**Kivi Product Position**

Kivi is not, in its essential character, a dictation utility that happens to be efficient - it is an ambient cognitive ledger: an instrument positioning the human voice as the primary record of memory and its output, rather than merely the primary technology of input. Its constituency is more than just businesses or organisations, but also every individual whose most valuable thought material is presently lost to the labour of transcription. Kivi's value lies precisely at this site of loss: to convert interstitial, unarticulated thought into either a durable personal archive or, where the thought merits it, an authoritative public form without demanding the separate, immense act of sitting down to compose. One should return to Kivi not because it types faster than a keyboard, but because it has become the site of retrospection.
